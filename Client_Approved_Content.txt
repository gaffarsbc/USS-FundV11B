UNITED SOCIAL SERVICES INC. (USS)

EXPANDING HOPE. HOUSING. & SUPPORT.

Building Pathways to Home, Stability, Opportunity & Stronger Communities.

United Social Services Inc. (USS) is a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation and social enterprise founded in 2018 to help individuals and families experiencing homelessness, housing instability, vulnerability, and other barriers to stability.

USS works to connect people with housing, essential services, supportive resources, education, transportation, workforce development, and opportunities that can help them move toward greater stability and independence.

We are serving people today while building the infrastructure, partnerships, programs, housing solutions, and sustainable resources needed to serve more people tomorrow.

WHY USS EXISTS

Everyone Deserves a Pathway Forward.

Housing instability and homelessness are rarely caused by one problem.

A person may need housing while also facing challenges involving transportation, employment, education, food security, family stability, disability, aging, or access to supportive resources.

USS believes that people should not have to navigate these challenges alone.

Our approach is centered on connecting housing + essential services + opportunity + community.

We work toward creating practical pathways that help individuals and families move from immediate need toward greater stability, independence, and long-term success.


WHO WE SERVE

USS seeks to serve individuals and families whose circumstances create significant barriers to housing stability, independence, opportunity, and well-being.

Our areas of focus include:

* Children and youth
* Transition-age youth and young adults
* Families experiencing housing instability
* Veterans and military families
* Older adults and seniors
* People with disabilities
* Individuals experiencing homelessness
* Vulnerable and underserved populations
* Individuals transitioning toward independent living

Our programs and services are developed and expanded according to community needs, available resources, partnerships, funding, regulatory requirements, and USS’s organizational capacity.

THE USS COMMUNITY HOUSING & SERVICES CAMPUS

OUR LONG-TERM CAPITAL VISION

USS is working toward developing a comprehensive Community Housing & Services Campus designed to bring housing and supportive services together within one coordinated community.

Initial Capital Campaign Goal:

$4,000,000

The $4 million goal represents USS’s initial capital campaign objective for advancing campus development.

INNOVATING HOW WE BUILD

DEVELOPING SMARTER, MORE FLEXIBLE INFRASTRUCTURE

USS is exploring cost-conscious and innovative approaches to campus development.

Potential approaches may include manufactured or mobile housing units for appropriate residential uses, where permitted by applicable zoning, building codes, funding requirements, licensing standards, and other regulations.

USS may also explore repurposing school buses and other mobile structures for appropriate non-residential purposes such as:

* Offices
* Training space
* Educational activities
* Technology labs
* Community resource centers
* Outreach operations
* Mobile service delivery
* Staff and volunteer operations

The purpose of exploring these approaches is to potentially allow USS to develop in phases, maximize available resources, and begin providing services without requiring every component of the ultimate campus vision to be completed simultaneously.
